Using SCIapp

Updated November 8, 2026

SCIapp is a personal care organization beta operated by Elad Rave. Questions about the service can be sent to eladrave@gmail.com. These usage terms describe the current beta.

Purpose and limits

SCIapp helps you organize information you enter, review care entries, track supplies and configure reminders. It does not prescribe medication, determine a safe dose, diagnose a condition, or replace a care professional. Frequency labels and reminder times reflect your entered plan. Photo measurements are experimental estimates that need your review.

Review your entries

Check medication names, dose labels, stock units, counts, timestamps and proposed changes before approving them. A reminder does not mean that care has been performed or a medication has been taken. Confirmed use is recorded separately. Correct an erroneous entry through the available correction controls or contact the operator.

Notifications and availability

Browser and device notifications can be delayed or missed. Hands-free listening is limited to supported browser behavior while the page is open. SCIapp is not an emergency alarm or monitoring service. Use your established care and emergency plans, and keep another reminder method when needed. The beta may change or be interrupted.

Your account and shared devices

Use an account you control and keep access credentials private. Sign out before sharing a device and check its microphone and notification settings. Enter information you are authorized to provide. Do not attempt to access another person's records or use the service to interfere with other users.

Data and connected services

The privacy notice describes the current handling of account and care information, voice, photos, AI requests and notifications. Google, Clerk, Cloudflare and browser services have their own terms and privacy policies. Caregiver invitations currently remain drafts; no caregiver access is granted. EHR, Health Connect and WhatsApp connections are not available in this beta.

Support and ending use

You can stop using the app and disable notifications or listening. Contact the operator for account export, correction or deletion requests; account ownership must be confirmed. No account-deletion button or paid subscription feature is currently implemented.
