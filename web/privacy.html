SCIapp privacy notice

Updated November 8, 2026

SCIapp is a personal care organization beta operated by Elad Rave. Contact eladrave@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. This notice describes the current app, rather than features that may be added later.

Information you provide

Your account can contain messages, medication and allergy lists, dosage labels, schedules, supply counts, confirmed medication use, catheterization entries, vital signs, photos, settings, and correction history. Caregiver invitation drafts can contain another person's email address; they currently do not send an invitation or grant access. A separate sample preview uses a temporary session and example starting stock.

Google sign-in and account information

Google sign-in uses basic identity information, such as your name, email address and profile picture, through Clerk to authenticate you and associate you with your SCIapp account. SCIapp's Google sign-in does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, or medical records. Clerk handles authentication and session information under its own privacy policy. Google processes sign-in under its privacy policy.

How care information is used and stored

SCIapp uses your entries to display your care journal, maintain supplies after confirmed actions, and calculate the reminders you configure. Account records are stored in Cloudflare D1, and uploaded photos are stored in private Cloudflare R2 storage. Authorized account requests are checked by the application before records or photos are returned. Cloudflare provides hosting, storage and AI processing; see its privacy policy.

AI, photos and voice

When you use AI features, your message, relevant recent conversation, medication and allergy information, settings, supply information, and submitted images may be processed by Cloudflare Workers AI to interpret your request or produce a response. Manual voice recordings submitted for transcription are processed to create text. The app does not intentionally save raw audio as a care record; transcripts and submitted messages may be saved in your conversation. Browser hands-free recognition may use the browser vendor's speech service, whose processing is governed by that provider's settings and policies. You can pause listening or turn it off. Muting spoken replies is a separate control.

Device notifications

If you enable notifications, the app stores a browser push subscription and delivery-attempt information. It uses the push service selected by your browser, which can involve Google, Mozilla or Apple. Notification messages contain a generic prompt to open SCIapp, rather than medication names, dosages or health readings. Delivery depends on your browser, device, network and provider. You can disable notifications for a device or in your browser settings.

Cookies, preferences and operational records

Authentication, preview sessions and the administrator portal use cookies. Administrator sessions expire after four hours, and an administrator audit log records administrative actions without passwords or private care information. Local browser storage holds preferences and an account identifier used to prevent a shared browser from inheriting another account's notification subscription. The service also uses IP-derived rate-limit identifiers, timestamps, request/error information and notification status for operation and abuse prevention. No advertising or advertising analytics service is intentionally integrated into the current app.

Access and service providers

The app uses information to provide the features described here. Authentication, hosting, AI and notification providers process information needed for their services. These providers may retain operational information under their own terms. Caregiver access, electronic medical-record connections and WhatsApp integration are not currently connected. SCIapp does not currently include a feature that sells care records or shares them with advertisers.

Personal memory and shared resources

Personal memory is automatic and always on for signed-in users. Their written chat messages can be stored as historical statements in D1 and indexed as embeddings in Cloudflare Vectorize using Workers AI. Your account has an isolated search namespace, with a separate ownership check before text is returned. Current confirmed care records take priority over remembered statements. You can ask to show, correct, forget, or clear remembered statements. Clearing removes stored memory text and schedules vector deletion; new messages continue to be remembered. There is no memory on/off setting. Original chat messages, care records, provider logs, and backups are separate. Administrators can publish source-linked educational excerpts into a separate shared collection. Private memory is never automatically published to it.

Retention, export and removal

There is currently no automatic retention or account-deletion schedule. Saved account information remains until removed through an available app feature or handled by the operator. Undo preserves an original entry and a correction in history. Deleting an uploaded photo removes it from app storage, but provider logs or backups may persist under their respective retention policies. Contact the operator to request an account export, correction, or deletion. Requests require confirmation of account ownership before changes are made.

Changes

As the beta changes, this notice may be updated to describe new processing. The date above identifies this version. Material new sharing features should be reviewed before you enable them.
