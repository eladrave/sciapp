SCIapp privacy notice

Updated November 8, 2026

SCIapp is a personal care organization beta operated by Elad Rave. Contact eladrave@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. This notice describes the current app, rather than features that may be added later.

Information you provide

Your account can contain messages, medication and allergy lists, dosage labels, schedules, supply counts, confirmed medication use, catheterization entries, vital signs, photos, settings, and correction history. Caregiver invitation drafts can contain another person's email address; they currently do not send an invitation or grant access. A separate sample preview uses a temporary session and example starting stock.

Google sign-in and account information

Google sign-in uses basic identity information, such as your name, email address and profile picture, through Clerk to authenticate you and associate you with your SCIapp account. SCIapp's Google sign-in does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, or medical records. Clerk handles authentication and session information under its own privacy policy. Google processes sign-in under its privacy policy.

How care information is used and stored

SCIapp uses your entries to display your care journal, maintain supplies after confirmed actions, and calculate the reminders you configure. Account records are stored in Cloudflare D1, and uploaded photos are stored in private Cloudflare R2 storage. Authorized account requests are checked by the application before records or photos are returned. Cloudflare provides hosting, storage and AI processing; see its privacy policy.

AI, photos and voice

When you use AI features, your message, relevant recent conversation, medication and allergy information, settings, supply information, and submitted images may be processed by Cloudflare Workers AI to interpret your request or produce a response. Manual voice recordings submitted for transcription are processed to create text. The app does not intentionally save raw audio as a care record; transcripts and submitted messages may be saved in your conversation. Browser hands-free recognition may use the browser vendor's speech service, whose processing is governed by that provider's settings and policies. You can pause listening or turn it off. When spoken replies are on, the text of each reply is sent to a Cloudflare Workers AI voice model (Deepgram Aura) to create the audio you hear; the audio is not saved. If that voice is unavailable, the reply is shown on screen without sound. Muting spoken replies is a separate control.

Connected health records

You may authorize one or more supported health systems through their own sign-in pages. SCIapp does not collect hospital portal passwords. OAuth credentials are encrypted with a server-held key and bound to your account and connection. Read-only FHIR imports include available medication requests, allergies, conditions, observations, encounters, diagnostic reports and document references. The original FHIR resources are encrypted in D1; limited source summaries are stored with your account to display them. Document references may not include full note contents. Imported text is used by AI when you ask to review it and may remain in your conversation history. Imports do not automatically change your medication plan, reminder times, inventory or confirmed care. Background import is off until you explicitly enable it and depends on continuing provider authorization. Disconnect removes SCIapp's saved authorization and stops future sync; you may also revoke the app in your provider portal. Deleting imported copies clears the separate record store and stops background sync; prior conversation excerpts, user-written memory, approved care entries and provider backups are separate and are not erased by that action. Exports include up to 100 recent imported summaries; source records can be opened individually. No records are sent back to the hospital.

Device notifications

If you enable notifications, the app stores a browser push subscription and delivery-attempt information. It uses the push service selected by your browser, which can involve Google, Mozilla or Apple. Notification messages contain a generic prompt to open SCIapp, rather than medication names, dosages or health readings. Delivery depends on your browser, device, network and provider. You can disable notifications for a device or in your browser settings.

Cookies, preferences and operational records

Authentication, preview sessions and the administrator portal use cookies. Administrator sessions expire after four hours, and an administrator audit log records administrative actions without passwords or private care information. Local browser storage holds preferences and an account identifier used to prevent a shared browser from inheriting another account's notification subscription. The service also uses IP-derived rate-limit identifiers, timestamps, request/error information and notification status for operation and abuse prevention. No advertising or advertising analytics service is intentionally integrated into the current app.

Access and service providers

The app uses information to provide the features described here. Authentication, hosting, AI and notification providers process information needed for their services. These providers may retain operational information under their own terms. Caregiver access and WhatsApp integration are not connected. Health system connections require vendor activation and your authorization. SCIapp does not currently include a feature that sells care records or shares them with advertisers.

Emergency call and location

The autonomic dysreflexia help shows a button that starts a phone call to 911, a second button for another number if you saved one in Settings, and your current location, so you can tell the dispatcher where you are. Your location is read by your device when that help opens or when you choose Update my location; it is shown on your screen only and is not sent to SCIapp or stored. If you choose Open this location in Maps, your coordinates are sent to Google Maps to show the map. A home address you choose to save in Settings is stored with your profile and shown on that panel.

Sharing with a caregiver

If you create a caregiver invitation link and someone accepts it, that person can see a view-only summary of your day: care that is due or not yet confirmed, the records you saved today, your intake and output totals, and low-supply warnings. They cannot see your photos, your conversation, your notes, your medication list, imported medical records or your settings, and they cannot change anything. You can remove a caregiver at any time in Settings, and a caregiver can stop following you. An invitation link works once and expires after seven days; anyone who has an unused link can accept it, so send it only to the person you mean.

AI providers

When an OpenAI-compatible provider is selected by the administrator, conversation text, relevant care context and retrieved excerpts are sent to that configured provider. Uploaded photos are sent to the configured image model for interpretation. Responses-format requests disable response storage (store:false); Chat Completions providers follow their own retention policies; this does not remove provider abuse-monitoring retention. Cloudflare still hosts app data, memory embeddings and transcription. Administrator settings can change the provider and models.

Personal memory and shared resources

Personal memory is automatic and always on for signed-in users. Their written chat messages can be stored as historical statements in D1 and indexed as embeddings in Cloudflare Vectorize using Workers AI. Your account has an isolated search namespace, with a separate ownership check before text is returned. Current confirmed care records take priority over remembered statements. You can ask to show, correct, forget, or clear remembered statements. Clearing removes stored memory text and schedules vector deletion; new messages continue to be remembered. There is no memory on/off setting. Original chat messages, care records, provider logs, and backups are separate. Administrators can upload original educational documents or presentations into a separate private Cloudflare R2 bucket, extract text, and publish reviewed source-linked resources into a separate shared collection. Uploaded originals can be downloaded by account or preview-session users while a linked resource is published. Video links use administrator-supplied summaries or transcripts; the app does not automatically download or watch linked video pages. Uploaded audio/video may be processed by Cloudflare Workers AI to generate a speech transcript for administrator review. Private memory is never automatically published to it.

Retention, export and removal

There is currently no automatic retention or account-deletion schedule. Saved account information remains until removed through an available app feature or handled by the operator. Undo preserves an original entry and a correction in history. Deleting an uploaded photo removes it from app storage, but provider logs or backups may persist under their respective retention policies. Contact the operator to request an account export, correction, or deletion. Requests require confirmation of account ownership before changes are made.

Changes

As the beta changes, this notice may be updated to describe new processing. The date above identifies this version. Material new sharing features should be reviewed before you enable them.
