# SCIapp implementation requirements

SCIapp is a companion for a person with a disability. The assistant helps with any question or task, using the published resources, the user's journal and what it remembers about them, and adds pictures or other media from the resources when it has them. Resources come first; when they are silent it still answers from general knowledge and says so. It never diagnoses or changes prescribed treatment, and health answers end with a short reminder to check with the doctor or care team. What users say about their preferences is remembered and used later.

This product is for people who may have limited use of their hands. Minimize required taps, typing and repeated questions in every feature.

- Group related care actions, especially medications sharing a scheduled time. Offer short contextual choices such as Took them all, Took only some and Not yet.
- Support the same workflow through ordinary voice/text, selectable answers and keyboard-accessible controls. Understand medicine names, numbered choices and all-except phrasing through the AI tools.
- Ask one essential question at a time. Reuse supplied facts and times. If the user already supplied a time or selection, proceed to the concrete review instead of asking again.
- Do not make the user approve what they just told you. Everyday records and settings the user states, selects or photographs (care done, amounts, vitals, doses, supplies, schedules, preferences) are saved at once and shown as a Saved card with one Undo; a spoken correction replaces the saved record. Only medication list imports, removing a medication and caregiver invitations wait for approval, as does everything when the account setting Ask me first is on. A medication group is one save and one Undo. Validate the entire group before applying any writes; retries must be idempotent.
- Never mark an unselected medication taken. Distinguish not taken yet, unconfirmed and actually taken. Missing records never establish adherence or clinical decisions.
- Use large controls, clear contrast, visible focus and useful screen-reader labels. Never require clicking a specific button when speaking an equivalent answer is possible.
- Camera capture timestamps are untrusted photo metadata, separate from upload time. Label missing-timezone assumptions. An explicit user time takes priority, and a record read from a photo says so and stays easy to correct or undo.
- Test with synthetic data in the isolated test account and Remote Browser. When explicitly authorized, private account copies may be used in that isolated account, with notifications disabled and the fixture removed afterward. Keep all real personal health data and credentials out of Git. Push every released version.

- Dismissing a reminder affects only its selected occurrence. Never infer taken, skipped or completed care, change stock, or disable future reminders from dismissal.
- A bag photo sent on its own records the scheduled catheterization it follows. A catheterization takes time, so a photo up to three hours after a scheduled time (and before the next one) counts for that occurrence. Record it directly with the read amount; do not ask whether it was done. The saved record, not the photo, clears the check-in.
- Open the conversation on the latest turns only. Earlier turns load in batches from a See earlier messages control that keeps the reader's place.
- Answer a question with the answer. How much, how many, when and whether get the number or fact in words, calculated by the server from approved records. Show journal cards only when the user asks to see or show records, photos or the journal.
- The Today page shows today only: reminders and records for the current local day in the account timezone, never tomorrow or later days.
- Spoken replies use only the AI voice through `/api/speech`. Never use the browser's built-in speech synthesis, not even as a fallback: when the AI voice fails, retry once and then stay silent with the reply on screen. Send the voice only reply text, never stored records in bulk, and keep mute, quiet hours and hands-free pausing in control of all audio.
- The admin resource form does the work itself: text and figures are read from an uploaded file automatically (and again for an earlier upload that has none). Show a control or field only when the admin must decide or type something.
- Suspected autonomic dysreflexia comes first: the AD help steps are returned at once, before any question or record, and are spoken when sound is on. Keep that text short, ordered and free of anything the user must read to act.
- Totals, trends and supply forecasts are calculated by the server from saved records (`worker/insights.js`). Word them as records, not health findings: a low total may be a missed record. Never suggest changing fluid intake, medication or care frequency.
- Caregiver sharing is view only and built from an explicit list of fields in `sharedDay`. Never add photos, conversation text, notes, imported medical records or settings to it, and never let a caregiver session read or write the owner's account directly. Invitation links are one-time, expire, and are stored only as a hash.
- The assistant answers questions. A question about the user's own records is answered from the journal; a knowledge question (what is, how do I, what is recommended, show me pictures about) is answered from published resources and personal memory, searching them again if needed. "Nothing in your journal" is never the answer to a knowledge question.
- Do not fix a knowledge answer for one question. Change the general mechanism (retrieval, the assistant's instructions, picture choice) and check it against a spread of different questions across the resources before and after.
- The emergency panel reads the device location only on the device and only for display. Never send it to the server, to the AI model, or to any third party service.
